## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added

- First playable BattleZero: drive a CardputerZero across a wireframe plain and fight
  waves of older Cardputers (the original, the v1.1 and the Adv), each with its own
  behaviour.
- Tilt the device left or right to steer, using the built-in motion sensor. F and X
  drive, Space or Enter fires, R recentres the tilt, T turns tilt off, Z and C steer by
  key.
- Score, three lives, rising waves and a saved high score.
- A simulator for the Mac so the game can be played without the device.
