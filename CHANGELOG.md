# Changelog

All notable changes to BattleZero are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Three new enemies from M5Stack's Core line-up, arriving from wave 6:
  - **Core Basic** (grey): armoured, takes two hits, and fires three-shot bursts.
  - **Core Fire** (rose): charges in close, fires a quick pair and swings away.
  - **Core2** (green): a sniper that keeps its distance; its shot is fast, but it
    winds up for a full second first.
- Each new wave names the most dangerous model in it.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added

- Sound through the speaker: your shot, enemy fire, a warning blip when an enemy is
  about to shoot, enemies breaking up, taking a hit, bumping into things, new waves,
  game over, and an engine note that rises with speed. Press M to mute.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added

- First playable BattleZero: drive a CardputerZero across a wireframe plain and fight
  waves of older Cardputers (the original, the v1.1 and the Adv), each with its own
  behaviour.
- Tilt the device left or right to steer, using the built-in motion sensor. F and X
  drive, Space or Enter fires, R recentres the tilt, T turns tilt off, Z and C steer by
  key.
- Score, three lives, rising waves and a saved high score.
- A simulator for the Mac so the game can be played without the device.
